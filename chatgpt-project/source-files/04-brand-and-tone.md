# Brand and Tone

## Brand Name

BlueGreen Guide

## Brand Meaning

The name connects blue spaces and green spaces:

- Blue spaces: water-centered outdoor places
- Green spaces: plant-rich or natural outdoor places

## Color Direction

Blue is primary. Green is secondary.

Current palette:

- Primary blue: `#176f8f`
- Dark blue: `#0f4f67`
- Soft blue: `#dceff5`
- Green accent: `#6f8f63`
- Dark green: `#4f7047`
- Background: `#eef6f8`
- Text: `#10252e`

## Voice

The voice should be:

- Useful
- Calm
- Clear
- Concrete
- Safety-aware
- Encouraging

Avoid:

- Hype
- Generic wellness copy
- Overpromising health benefits
- Unsupported claims
- Extreme adventure language

## UI Feel

The app should feel map-first, relaxed, and practical. It should help users choose where to go without overwhelming them.
