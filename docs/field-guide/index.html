01 · Orient

Know what the markers mean.

Blue identifies water places and water activities. Green identifies land places and land activities. Neutral styling is used for universal amenities and attributes such as parking, restrooms, accessibility, rentals, and similar information.

02 · Understand

Open the place details.

Use the place detail panel to review the fuller description, general planning fields, representative photo information, source links, water-body context, and verification notes.

Understand place details →

03 · Verify

Read the status before relying on a detail.

Needs Verification means one or more material fields have not been individually checked against a current, reliable source. Verification status is about confidence in the available place information, not whether the place itself is safe or suitable.

About verification →

04 · Check

Conditions and access can change.

Review current weather, water conditions, access information, local advisories, and official sources before your trip. Access, parking, fees, hours, facilities, local regulations, and other practical details can change.

Safety guidance →

BlueGreen Guide is a planning aid, not a safety service. Current conditions and official local information take priority over general planning guidance.
