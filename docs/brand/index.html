Usage rules

Keep the identity activity-neutral

Do not add paddleboards, kayaks, hikers, animals, or activity-specific equipment to the permanent brand logo. Those belong in the Wayfinding System.

Use the right variant

Primary for general use, dark-mode variants on dark surfaces, stacked for portrait/square placements, and the mark or app icon where space is limited.

Do not distort

Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, or recolor outside the approved package.
