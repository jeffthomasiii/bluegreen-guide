# Brand Guide

## Name

**BlueGreen Guide**

## Meaning

The name comes from the relationship between blue spaces and green spaces:

- Blue spaces: oceans, rivers, lakes, reservoirs, bays, harbors, and other water-centered places.
- Green spaces: parks, woods, gardens, trails, campgrounds, and other plant-rich outdoor areas.

The app begins with paddleboarding and kayaking launch points, but the name leaves room for broader outdoor wellness and recreation discovery.

## Tone

Use language that is:

- Clear
- Practical
- Calm
- Specific
- Encouraging
- Safety-aware

Avoid language that feels:

- Overly clinical
- Overly spiritualized
- Overly influencer-like
- Extreme-sport focused
- Vague wellness-speak

## Visual Direction

Blue is the primary color because the app begins with blue-space discovery. Green is the secondary accent because the larger product idea includes restorative natural spaces.

Current palette:

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Primary blue | `#176f8f` |
| Dark blue | `#0f4f67` |
| Soft blue | `#dceff5` |
