# BlueGreen Guide FAQ

## Is BlueGreen Guide nationwide?

Not yet. Version 1.0 is a curated proof of concept with a focused launch collection.

## Why keep the dataset smaller for v1.0?

Because the goal of v1.0 is to prove the experience: map-first discovery, clear filters, useful launch cards, detail views, and responsible verification language.

## Does Beginner mean safe?

No. Beginner means generally more approachable based on available information. Conditions vary and must be checked before going.

## Are weather, tide, and wind live?

No. Live conditions belong to a later planning phase.

## Can I rely on access, parking, or fee information?

Only when verified by current official sources. If information is unknown or marked as needing verification, check the official source before going.

## Can users add locations or photos?

Not in v1.0. User submissions are a possible future community-layer feature.
