Verification & sources

BlueGreen Guide favors transparency over assumptions. Verification language tells you how much confidence to place in the available information.

What “Needs Verification” means

One or more material fields have not been individually checked against a current reliable source. This can include access rules, parking, fees, hours, facilities, local regulations, or other practical details.

What verification does not mean

Verification status reflects confidence in the available place information. It is not a rating of the quality of a place and it is not a guarantee that conditions are safe or suitable.

Official sources still have limits

An official source may confirm only part of a record. A source link does not automatically verify every field shown for that place.

When in doubt: use the current official park, harbor, marina, city, county, or agency source before relying on a changing detail.
