Before you go

Check before you go

BlueGreen Guide is a planning aid. It does not guarantee access, conditions, or safety.

Planning, not a safety guarantee. Current conditions and official local information take priority over general planning guidance.

Review changing information

Before leaving, check current weather and water conditions, access information, closures, fees, local rules, and any relevant official advisories.

Conditions vary

A place that is generally suitable at one time can become less suitable because of wind, water movement, traffic, closures, equipment, or personal circumstances.

Use official sources for material decisions

When BlueGreen Guide marks a detail as unknown or needing verification, use the current official source before relying on it.
