Discover

Explore

The map is the central discovery tool. Use it with the result list and curated collections to move through an area and narrow what you are viewing.

Use the map

Drag to pan, zoom in and out, select markers, use Fit all places to reset the view, or show only launch points inside the current map area.

Search

Search by place, region, activity, water type, amenity, water body, tag, or descriptive text. Examples include Newport, Dana Point Harbor, beginner, rentals, scenic, or calm water.

Filters

Filter by region, skill, water activity, and maximum difficulty. Filters can be combined with search and curated collections to create a smaller planning list.

Curated collections

Collections provide a quick starting point for common needs. Selecting one filters the map and result list. Clear the collection to return to the full launch map.

Place details

Open a place detail panel to review the fuller description, general planning fields, representative photo information, source links, water-body context, and verification notes.

Verification

BlueGreen Guide intentionally favors transparency over assumptions. Information marked Needs Verification should be confirmed through official park, harbor, marina, city, county, or agency sources before use. Verification status reflects confidence in the available place information, not the quality of the place or a guarantee that conditions are suitable.

Sources

An official source may confirm only part of a record. Access rules, parking, fees, hours, facilities, local regulations, and other practical details can change, so material details that have not been individually checked remain clearly identified.

Check before you go

Planning, not a safety guarantee. Always review official sources, current conditions, access information, and local advisories before going.
