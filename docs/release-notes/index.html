BlueGreen Guide v1.0

The first public proof of concept establishes a calm, practical, map-first experience for discovering curated paddleboarding and kayaking launch points.

Summary

BlueGreen Guide v1.0 proves the core product direction: a curated outdoor discovery app that helps people find launch points, compare practical information, and follow verification links without adding accounts, reviews, live conditions, or other advanced features too early.

Highlights

Discovery

Map and curated places

Interactive Leaflet and OpenStreetMap launch map with a curated collection of launch points.

Planning

Search, filters, and cards

Search across launch names, regions, tags, activities, amenities, and descriptions, with filters for region, skill, activity, and maximum difficulty.

Details

Launch information

Launch cards and detail views provide practical summaries, descriptions, source links, and verification notes.

Responsive

Mobile-friendly layout

The interface adapts for smaller screens while preserving the map, search, filters, results, and place details.

Documentation

Guides and project context

Documentation home, Field Guide & User Manual, Quick Start, release notes, roadmap, FAQ content, and screenshot system.

Scope note

Curated by design: This release intentionally keeps the dataset limited rather than attempting nationwide coverage. The purpose is to validate the product experience and data model before expanding the number of places or adding more complex services.

Not included yet

These capabilities remain outside the Phase 1 proof of concept and should be considered in the context of the roadmap.

Live conditions

Weather, wind, tides, water temperature, water quality, and other changing conditions are not currently integrated.

Personal accounts

Accounts, saved places, favorites, and personal trip notes are not included.

Community content

Ratings, reviews, user-submitted photos, and suggested edits are not included.

AI recommendations

Natural-language place recommendations and automated trip planning belong to a later phase.

Known limitation: BlueGreen Guide is a planning aid, not a live conditions or safety service. Access, rules, fees, closures, and environmental conditions can change after a place was last reviewed.
