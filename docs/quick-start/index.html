Planning, not a safety guarantee: BlueGreen Guide summarizes general information. Conditions, access, fees, closures, hazards, and local rules can change. Check official sources and current conditions before heading out.

1. Open the map

Map-first Curated launches

Start at the BlueGreen Guide home page. On desktop, the app opens with planning tools and results beside the interactive map. On smaller screens, the same information stacks into a mobile-friendly layout.

2. Search for a place or feature

Use Search Blue Spaces to look for a place, region, activity, water type, amenity, tag, or descriptive feature.

Places and regions

Try searches such as Newport, Dana Point, or Harbor.

Experience and features

Try Beginner, Rentals, Lake, Scenic, or Calm Water.

3. Filter the results

Use the available filters to narrow the current list. Region, skill, activity, and maximum difficulty can be combined with search or a curated collection.

Region Skill Activity Max difficulty

4. Compare launch cards

Each launch card summarizes the place, water type, general skill level, difficulty, popularity, best general time, activities, amenities, tags, and verification status. Use the cards to build a short list rather than treating any single label as a guarantee.

5. Open the details

Select View details for a fuller description, water-body context, representative photo information, planning fields, verification notes, and source links where available.

6. Check before going

Confirm access

Check official park, harbor, marina, city, county, or agency sources for current access, parking, fees, closures, and rules.

Review conditions

Check current weather, wind, tides where relevant, water conditions, water quality, and any local advisories before leaving.

Plan for your ability

Consider your experience, equipment, companions, and comfort level. A general “Beginner” label does not mean conditions are safe for every person or every day.
