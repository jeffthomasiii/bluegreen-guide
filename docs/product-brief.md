# Product Brief

## Working Name

**BlueGreen Guide**

## One-Sentence Concept

BlueGreen Guide helps people find nearby blue-space and green-space outdoor experiences, beginning with paddleboarding and kayaking launch points.

## Product Thesis

People often want to get outside, relax, and spend time near water, but they do not always know where to go, what conditions matter, or whether a location fits their skill level. BlueGreen Guide starts as a curated launch-point map and can grow into a recommendation engine for restorative outdoor water experiences.

## Blue Spaces

Blue spaces are places shaped by water, such as:

- Oceans
- Beaches
- Bays
- Rivers
- Lakes
- Reservoirs
- Lagoons
- Harbors

## Green Spaces

Green spaces are natural or planted outdoor places, such as:

- Parks
- Woods
- Gardens
- Trails
- Shoreline open space
- Campgrounds
- Wildlife areas

## Initial Audience

- Beginner and casual paddleboarders
- Kayakers looking for easier launch options
- People planning short outdoor reset trips
- Families and couples looking for accessible water-based outings
- Later: clubs, guides, outfitters, and local paddling communities

## Initial Geography

Phase 1 seed data focuses on:

- Southern California
- Central California
- Nevada
- Arizona

## Positioning

BlueGreen Guide should feel practical, calm, and trustworthy. It should avoid sounding like a generic wellness app or a hardcore outdoor performance app. The tone should be useful, clear, encouraging, and specific.

## Long-Term Direction

The product can grow from a static launch map into an outdoor planning platform:

- Conditions-aware recommendations
- Weather, wind, tide, and water-temperature integrations
- Community photos and reviews
- Saved favorite places
- Suggested paddle routes
- Trip logging
- AI-assisted recommendations
- Local club and outfitter discovery
