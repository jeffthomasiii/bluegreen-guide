What Comes Next · Roadmap

Grow the guide one useful phase at a time.

The roadmap begins with a curated launch map, then adds richer place details, conditions, community contributions, recommendations, and a broader blue-space and green-space ecosystem.

Open the Current App Review the Current Release
Start with blue spacesLaunch points establish the first practical use case
Expand to green spacesBroaden the guide after the core structure is proven
Verify before scalingImprove data quality and usefulness before adding volume
Roadmap principle: Each phase should deliver a useful, understandable improvement without requiring a large rewrite or introducing advanced features before the supporting data and workflows are ready.

Phase 1 — Curated Launch Map

Current phase v1.0 package prepared

Build and refine the static proof of concept around paddleboarding and kayaking launch points.

Core experience

Interactive map, curated launch data, search, filters, launch cards, and a detail panel.

Trust and clarity

Source links, verification fields, practical descriptions, and safety-aware language.

Usability

Responsive layout, mobile planning, documentation, and a coherent blue-primary design system.

Phase 2 — Place Detail Pages

Make each launch point useful enough for real planning by adding structured, source-aware place information.

Entry type Parking Fees Restrooms Rentals Dog policy Accessibility Hazards Official links Last verified

Phase 3 — Conditions and Planning

Help users decide when to go by connecting places with relevant, clearly sourced planning information.

Weather and wind

Current or forecast conditions that materially affect outdoor planning.

Water conditions

Tides where relevant, water temperature, and seasonal access notes.

Daylight

Sunrise and sunset information for practical trip timing.

Phase 4 — Community Layer

Let the guide improve through structured user contributions while preserving source quality and moderation.

Ratings Reviews Photos Suggested edits Favorite places Trip notes

Phase 5 — AI Recommendations

Allow users to describe what they need in natural language and receive suggestions based on the structured place data, distance, skill, water type, conditions, and practical constraints.

Example: Find somewhere within an hour of Riverside that is beginner-friendly, calm in the morning, dog-friendly, and not too crowded.

Phase 6 — Routes and Ecosystem

Expand beyond individual launch points into paddle routes, estimated duration, trip logging, local clubs, outfitters, lessons, shareable trip plans, and broader blue-space and green-space discovery.

Roadmap, not a promise: Scope and sequence may change as the prototype is tested, data quality improves, and the most useful next step becomes clearer.
